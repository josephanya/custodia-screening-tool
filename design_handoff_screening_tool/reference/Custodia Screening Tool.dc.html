FLAG URGENT EMERGENCY
Lower risk Higher risk Routine (plain text)

FLAG = diagnosed high risk (square, navy). Urgent = outlined pill. Emergency = solid pill. Readable in greyscale.

Accessibility
  • All text ≥ 4.5:1. #8597AA is reserved for placeholders and disabled states.
  • Focus: 3px #4F9EEA outline, 3px offset, on every interactive element. Never removed.
  • Tap targets ≥ 56px in the public flow, ≥ 44px in the dashboard.
  • Progress uses role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow/max and a text label "Question 3 of 11". Move focus to the new question heading on Next.
  • Option tiles are a radiogroup; arrow keys move, Space selects. Yes/No and single-select share the same pattern.
  • Urgent and emergency banners use role="alert" and are the first thing on the result page.
  • Dashboard rows are links (Enter opens); urgency is announced as text, not inferred from row colour.
Interaction
  • Progress: bar animates width over 300ms ease-out; count updates when the pathway is chosen.
  • Selection: tap selects instantly (tick + border). Yes/No questions auto-advance after 250ms; numeric and multi-option wait for Next.
  • Next is disabled until a valid answer exists. Disabled state keeps its label so the action stays clear.
  • Validation shows on Next or blur, never while typing. Message sits under the field with an icon.
  • Loading: "See my result" becomes "Scoring…" with a spinner; Back is disabled until scoring completes.
  • Submission errors appear in a red error box above the controls; answers are kept.
